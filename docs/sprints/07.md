# Sprint 07 [MVP]: Durable jobs and revision-safe asset requests

## Outcome
Durable jobs and revision-safe asset requests.

## Queue
Sprint 07 of 19 · MVP. Take the lowest-numbered open issue whose title starts with `Sprint`. Complete its acceptance criteria before closing it and advancing. Sprint numbers express delivery order, not fixed calendar dates. No agent delegation is required by this ticket.

## Prerequisites
- Sprint 06: Revision-aware edits, approval, and scoped layer locks

## Scope
- Introduce queued/running/succeeded/failed/cancelled jobs, progress/diagnostics, status retrieval and bounded local execution.
- Snapshot project revision and effective inputs at submission; duplicate idempotency keys reuse jobs.
- Support selected-layer asset requests with renderer capability discovery and draft candidates separate from accepted artwork.
- Coordinate processes through the local job store; document crash recovery and ownership of execution, with no phantom running jobs after owner death.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A job completing after edits retains its original revision and cannot silently replace newer selected artwork.
- [ ] Duplicate submissions do not duplicate work; cancellation/failure never publish successful incomplete output.
- [ ] Available static/text/SVG requests work; unavailable generative capabilities produce structured errors.
- [ ] Restart/recovery tests cover interrupted jobs and bounded execution; stale candidate selection fails safely.
- [ ] Implementation, relevant tests, and usage documentation are in the repository; report verification and remaining limitations before closing.

## Ownership boundaries
internal/jobs/, internal/build/, app/ asset/job operations

## Out of scope
ComfyUI transport, remote/distributed scheduling.

## Architecture requirements
Go; command factories with invocation-local Viper and pointer-based pre-run initialization. CLI/MCP/web call shared application services. Composition drives renderer requests; stable project YAML remains backend-neutral. Preserve manual overrides and lock policy.
